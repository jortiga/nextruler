Common questions

How do I play?

  • Place buildings on either half of the map; every building spawns troops that fight on their own
  • One-tap building, drag to pan, pinch to zoom
  • Survive 8:00 without letting either HQ fall

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. The Next Ruler has no accounts or cloud saves, so progress can't be moved to another phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Why are there ads?

The Next Ruler is free and shows full-screen ads between games through Google AdMob. Ads never appear during play. They keep the game free.

How do I change my ad choices?

If you are in a region where consent is required, the game asks before showing personalised ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time. On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by phone). On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
